# Chess Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is the difference between Checkmate and Stalemate?

- **Checkmate**: The King is currently in check, and there is no legal move to escape check. The attacking player wins immediately.
- **Stalemate**: The player whose turn it is has no legal move, but their King is **not** in check. The game ends immediately in a draw (tie).

---

## 2. When is Castling illegal?

Castling is illegal if:
1. The King or the chosen Rook has already moved at any point during the game.
2. There are pieces standing between the King and the Rook.
3. The King is currently in check.
4. The King would pass through or land on a square under attack by an enemy piece.

---

## 3. How does En Passant work?

En passant is a special pawn capture that occurs when an opponent moves a pawn two squares forward from its starting rank, landing directly adjacent to your pawn. You may capture that pawn diagonally as if it had only moved one square forward. **This capture must be made on the very next turn.**

---

## 4. What is the Elo Rating System?

The Elo rating system is a statistical method for calculating relative skill levels of chess players.
- A beginner rating is typically around 800–1000.
- An intermediate club player ranges from 1400 to 1800.
- A Master starts around 2200.
- A Grandmaster (GM) is typically 2500+.
- The highest rating ever achieved in classical chess is 2882 by Magnus Carlsen.

---

## 5. What are the most popular first moves in chess?

- **`1.e4` (King's Pawn)**: Sharp, tactical, opens lines quickly for the Queen and light-squared Bishop ("Best by test" – Bobby Fischer).
- **`1.d4` (Queen's Pawn)**: Strategic, solid, defends d4 with the Queen.
- **`1.Nf3` (Reti Opening)**: Flexible, delays committing central pawns.
- **`1.c4` (English Opening)**: Fights for d5 controls from the flank.

---

## 6. What is the Lucena Position?

The Lucena position is a foundational winning endgame scenario in Rook endgames where the attacker has a pawn on the 7th rank with the King in front of it. The attacker wins by using the "building a bridge" technique with the Rook on the 4th rank to block enemy checks.

---

## 7. What is the strongest chess engine today?

**Stockfish** (enhanced with NNUE evaluation) is currently the world's strongest chess engine, with an estimated rating well over 3500 Elo.

---

## 8. Can a player promote a pawn to a second Queen?

Yes! There is no restriction on how many Queens or other pieces a player can have on the board through pawn promotion. A player could theoretically have up to 9 Queens.
